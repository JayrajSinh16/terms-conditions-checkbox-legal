Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Terms & Condition Checkbox (the "App") collects, uses, and shares information when you install or use the App in connection with your Shopify-supported store.

1. Personal Information the App Collects

When you install the App, we are automatically able to access certain types of information from your Shopify account via the API:

  • Merchant Information: Your shop domain, store name, and the email address associated with your Shopify account.
  • App Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the App's settings dashboard.

2. Customer Information

The App functions as a storefront widget. To protect your customers' privacy:

  • We do not collect, store, or process Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from your store's customers (such as names, physical addresses, or credit card details).
  • The App's primary function is to block the checkout flow until the checkbox is ticked; this logic typically runs locally on your storefront.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect from you to:

  • Provide the App's core functionality and services.
  • Communicate with you regarding support requests or technical updates.
  • Facilitate billing via the Shopify Billing API.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only to comply with applicable laws, to respond to a subpoena, or to protect our rights.

5. Data Retention

When you uninstall the App, we retain your store information for a period of 48 hours to allow for easy re-installation. After this period, your data is purged from our active records in accordance with Shopify’s data protection requirements.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time in order to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.
